# Docker images run Node 24

Date: 2026-11-06 · Status: accepted

## Context

Both Dockerfiles were on `node:20-alpine`, which is out of support and contradicted `engines.node`
`>=24` (see `package-json-choices`) and CI.

## Decision

- `Dockerfile` and `Dockerfile.dev` use `node:24-alpine`, the same major as `engines` and CI.

## Consequences

- This settles the consequence in `package-json-choices` that the Dockerfiles stay on Node 20 until
  MY-222: they are now on Node 24.
- A Node major bump moves `engines`, CI and both Dockerfiles together.
